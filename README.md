About
Cybersecurity professional moving into security operations after 9+ years in enterprise IT. At Orsted, I administered identity lifecycle for 500+ Microsoft Entra ID accounts (MFA, Conditional Access, privileged-access procedures), escalated account-compromise indicators and unauthorized authentication attempts, and wrote the team's authentication and privilege-escalation runbooks. At Amazon, I supported Linux fleets and network infrastructure under formal change control.

I am finishing a B.S. in Cybersecurity & Information Assurance at WGU (expected November 2026) and building SOC, vulnerability management, and identity security labs.
